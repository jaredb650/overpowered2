# DESERT RAID — mission plan

Status: **plan agreed with the owner, 2026-11-22. Nothing built yet.** Next step: the full script (every line, section by
section) for the owner's OK before any building starts.

## The idea

An ally drives you across open desert to a huge bandit shantytown. You tear its front wall off with the truck's heavy gun,
then storm it on foot with a squad of friendlies, cutting your own way through the shacks. At the end you corner the warlord
for his last speech, and on the way out the whole town is flattened from the air.

Feel: old desert raiding-party photos. Open trucks with mounted guns, scarves and goggles, dust, low gunships.

## The villain: Hadrek Quist, "the Salt King"

- **A bandit warlord. No Council story in this mission.** These are bandits, and you're there to deal with them.
- His bandits are **the Saltbloods**: scarves, goggles, welded scrap armour, armed trucks, bikes.
- His town, **Surplus**, is miles of rusted walls, towers and shacks, with a crashed cargo plane in the middle where he lives.
- **He's frightening, not funny.** He hangs bodies off the walls and controls the town's only water, which keeps everyone
  in line. He speaks slowly and calmly and doesn't threaten twice.
- **He runs the level.** Loudspeakers on poles cover the town and he talks through them the whole time. He sends trucks at
  you, opens and shuts gates, offers a reward for your head, drops the water tower on you, and finally runs.

## Your side

- **Three open trucks with guns** (the truck from Foreclosure), with an **ally driving each one on a set path. The player
  never drives.** In solo you're on the heavy gun. In co-op, other players take the passenger seats and spare guns.
- **Two gunships** escort the convoy and drop squads of friendlies. **They only strike at scripted moments**, never freely,
  so the set is still standing when the player gets to it.
- **Friendly squads storm the town on foot with you.** It's a full invasion. They push streets, take buildings and die.
  There are enough bandits that the friendlies never clear an area for you.
- **Sergeant Marr** leads on the radio, in short, clear lines.

## Destruction: almost everything except the plane

- **Sheet-metal walls:** bullets punch holes; heavy guns tear the panels off.
- **Shacks:** shoot out the posts and they fold in, along with whoever is inside.
- **Guard towers:** shoot the legs and they tip over and crash down.
- **Sandbag and crate walls** crumble. **Fuel drums** burst into fire. **Awnings, washing lines, water tanks, doors** all
  come down.
- **The plane is fully solid** (kept simple). A few big rock or concrete pieces stay solid too, as fixed points to find
  your way by.
- **The rubble stays where it falls.**
- **Cut your own way through:** gates still separate the big sections, but inside the shanty section (the Warren) there's
  no single path. You can shoot a hole through a shack wall and walk through it.
- It must **feel great to shoot**: every hit visibly does something (sparks, dents, flapping panels, dust, heavy sound).
  Hitting a solid wall is never what most shots feel like.

## The route: six sections

### 1. The Approach (dawn, open desert)
- The trucks bounce across the dunes with the two gunships low overhead. You're on the heavy gun in the middle truck.
- You warm up on lookout posts and a few bandit bikes and trucks coming over the dunes.
- Quist's first words, calm, over the radio: "You're a long way from water. Turn around and I'll let you die of thirst
  instead."
- The town rises on the horizon, with the plane on top.

### 2. The Wall: the shooting gallery (the money section)
- The convoy drives a slow loop along the outer wall, and you tear it apart: panels, towers, sandbag posts, drums.
- Quist sends out reinforcements: trucks come out of the side gates, and men run along walkways behind the wall.
  **You shoot them through the wall.** Muzzle flashes and shadows through the gaps show where they are.
- **The main gate is the one thing you can't shoot through:** welded plate with a bulldozer blade on the front. Quist: "You
  can scratch my walls all day. That gate held off an army."
- **Scripted strike:** Marr calls it in, and a short camera cut shows a Warthog gun run tearing the gate open. The gunships
  drop the first friendly squads into the gap. Marr: "Gate's down. On foot. Go."
- This section should be the most enemies at once and the most things breaking in the game so far.

### 3. The Warren (the shantytown on foot)
- Not called "the market": the game already has a market area.
- Tight alleys, two-storey shacks, rooftops, washing lines, awnings. **This is the "cut your own way" section.** Shack walls
  are paper to you, and to them. You can go through buildings, over the roofs (wall run, jump the gaps) or bring the roofs
  down on people.
- Friendly squads fight up the alleys beside you.
- **Quist's move:** a bounty on the speakers. "A year of water to whoever brings me one of their heads." Bandits pour in
  from everywhere. It's a big chaotic crowd fight.
- **Optional mounted minigun** in a rooftop nest. You don't need it to progress. Take it and you can saw a whole street of
  shacks flat while bandits charge you.

### 4. The Pump Yard
- Big tanks, pipes and a tall water tower. It's cleaner and better guarded than the Warren, because the water is the most
  valuable thing he has.
- **Quist's move:** as you cross, he drops the water tower across your path. The flood knocks everyone flat and blocks the
  way forward.
- **You drop into the sewer:** dark, wet, echoing. The **Thermal brain chip** finds the bandits waiting in the dark. Quist
  can't see you down there either, and it shows in his voice.
- You come up inside his base, under the plane.

### 5. The Plane: his base and the ending
- It's built around the crashed plane hull (solid). His best men are here in heavier welded armour.
- **Quist runs like a coward.** He shuts doors behind him and sends his men back at you. You chase him through the hull.
- **Cornered at the tail, he starts his last speech (15 to 20 seconds).** What happens depends on the player:

| What the player does | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Hold E on him before the speech ends** | **Captured.** Zip-tied, more pay. |
| **Tap E** | He answers with a line. You can talk to him. |
| **Shoot him during the speech** | **Killed.** The speech is cut off mid-word. |
| **Let him finish while within 8 m** | He pulls the pin on a hidden grenade to take you with him. Short warning first, so it's fair. Run or shoot him. |
| **Let him finish from more than 8 m** | He pulls a pistol and **shoots himself**, using the existing (rarely seen) pistol suicide animation. |

- The tension is that capturing him means getting close, and getting close is exactly when he uses the grenade. You have
  to grab him before he finishes.

### 6. The Exfil and the ending cutscene
- The trucks smash through the rubble to pick you up. Back on the heavy gun, every Saltblood left chases you into the
  desert.
- **Ending cutscene:** as the convoy drives away, wave after wave of airstrikes, Warthog runs and gunship strikes flatten
  the town completely. Nothing is left standing but the plane. It's the scenic final shot.
- If Quist was captured, he sits in the truck with you and has a few last lines as he watches his town go.

## Quist's speech (first draft)

> "Eleven years I kept this town alive. Water, food, order. You think the people here hate me? They *need* me. When you
> leave, they'll be killing each other over a bucket by morning. You didn't come here to free anyone. You came to break
> something. Well. Look around. You did."

Tap-E replies (draft):
- "Who sent you? Doesn't matter. Somebody always does."
- "No. I won't beg."

Ending lines (draft):
- Pistol: "Not by your hand." *(shot)*
- Grenade: "Come closer. I'll tell you a secret."

## Decisions (owner, 2026-11-22)

- Kill or capture: **the player chooses** (plus the grenade or pistol endings if they wait).
- Trucks: **an ally drives a set path**; the player never drives.
- Destruction: **almost everything** (walls, towers, shacks, the Warren); **the plane is fully solid**.
- Villain: **a frightening desert bandit warlord**; no Council story in this mission.
- Gunships and air: **scripted strikes only** (the gate, the ending cutscene), so the set survives until the player
  reaches it.
- Section 3 is **not called the market**; its mounted minigun is **optional**.
- Speech **15 to 20 s**; grenade range **8 m**.
- Exfil **kept**, ending on the town being flattened from the air.

## What it will need (guesses, the code hasn't been checked yet)

1. A desert map much bigger than Foreclosure, at least for the open stretches.
2. A shanty destruction kit: wall panels, posts, towers, shacks, drums, awnings that break into big chunks and stay as
   rubble. This is the biggest new job.
3. Saltblood enemy look; bandit trucks, bikes.
4. The Foreclosure truck with a gunner seat, ally drivers on set paths, three-truck convoy.
5. Gunships that drop friendlies and strike on cue; a scripted Warthog gate run; the ending air-strike cutscene.
6. Friendly assault squads that push through the town.
7. Quist's loudspeaker voice across the map; the water tower drop and flood; the sewer section.
8. The last-speech ending: interruptible speech, tap-E replies, hold-E capture, 8 m grenade check, pistol suicide.
9. An optional mounted minigun.
