# Weapon ideas backlog (parked, 2026-11-17)

The owner picked these from a brainstorm, to come back to later. Not scheduled.
Rejected in the same brainstorm, don't propose again: saw-blade launcher (cliché), arc/tesla chain gun (not satisfying).

## 1. Riot shield, a pistol mod
- One mod that fits ANY pistol (Rivet, Ultramag, Judge, Autoraffica, and future pistols). It lives on the gun, so it needs no slot of its own.
- With it fitted, the off hand holds a shield: you lose ADS (aim does nothing or only zooms), the shield blocks frontal fire, and a shield bash is possible.
- Excludes akimbo.
- Commitment: fitting it changes how that pistol plays, so it's a choice, not a free upgrade.

## 2. Microwave emitter (new weapon), the owner's favourite
- A beam or cone that cooks enemies from the inside: they stagger, scream, swell, then pop.
- Slow and horrible. Different gore: burns, steam, boiling, a pop.
- Open questions: range, heat build-up or overheat, armour interaction (does it ignore plates?), and the look of the beam (heat shimmer: the ShimmerLayer exists).

## 3. Stakegun ammo overhaul: hold R cycles ammo types
- **Change the bind:** ammo or fire-mode switching moves from right click to **hold R**, the same as the HMG, Gimlet and Harrow. That frees right click.
- **Ammo types, cycled with hold R:**
  1. Regular stake (existing).
  2. Exploding bolt (existing).
  3. **Tether bolt (mod):** fire it into a surface and it reels YOU in. It's a movement tool: tether to a wall, stick to it, then wall-climb up. It can reach places the parkour alone can't, which makes it a big movement changer. Check how it fits wall cling, climb and Jump Jet.
  4. **Sticky charge (mod):** reuses the exploding-bolt logic, but the stakes stick and don't go off. **Right click detonates them all while the Stakegun is out.** Switching away from the Stakegun detonates them automatically, so remote control only works while it's in your hands.
- Each new ammo is a shop mod with the usual row, cheat row and test.

## 4. Throwing knives (rework the shiv)
- The existing shiv (a weak katana with no combos) becomes the **throwing knife**, a secondary in the melee slot.
- **Left click throws, right click slashes** (keep the old shiv slash).
- Thrown knives cause bleeding, a headshot is an instant kill, and knives can be picked back up from bodies.
- Should feel satisfying: a quick throw, a thunk sound, a visible knife in the target.
